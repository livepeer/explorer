1. Validator set #804

Filter tabs for who’s in the set. Light rows. Detail has the membership pill and how often they filed.

↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Set rule used here. Active set = the 33 highest-staked node operators who have registered. A higher-staked node that has not registered is “Could join”. The next registered node below the cutoff is “Next in line”. Starting size is 33. Governance can change it later.
  • Don’t repeat status three times. Filter tabs for who’s in the set. List rows: name, rank, address, and one muted fact (Active / Occasional / Inactive inside the set, or why they are outside). Detail has one membership pill and the full “how often they filed” sentence with a help tip.
  • Default selection on wide. First row in the current filter is selected on load and when the filter changes, so the detail pane isn’t empty. A deep link still wins when present.
  • Filed scores jump to that node. Each name under “Scores they filed” opens that validator’s detail (and switches the filter if needed).
  • List and detail. Split on purpose. No dual membership and filing chips on rows. See #844.
  • Blanks are “No score”. Those rows are left out of the median in this mock. See open questions.
  • Filing habit. Active, Occasional, and Inactive mean how often they filed scores in the last 7 rounds. Same words as the tip. Muted under the name and again as the detail sentence. Slate is Inactive and still paid.
  • Pay follows delegated stake. 1% of new LPT, weighted by delegated stake inside the set, then the node’s usual cut. Emissions are still a draft, so no dollar figure.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Words for mid-range scores. This sample is 0.95, so the card says honest. The draft has no bands. Recommended default: say the share of the reward in words (“keeps 40%”) and reserve “Honest” for a score at or very near 1. Owner: Validator Track.
  • What a missing score means. Recommended default: show “No score” and omit it from the median. Owner: Validator Track.
  • How the 1% is split. The parameter table weights by delegated stake, then the node’s cut. Another sentence can be read as an equal split across the set. Recommended default: stake-weighted, then the usual cut. Owner: Validator Track.
  • Health, and pay for quiet validators. Recommended default: keep Active / Occasional / Inactive as how often they filed. Leave pay as drawn until the Validator Track sets a minimum filing rule. Owner: Validator Track.
  • A validator scoring their own node. Recommended default: it counts like any other score, one of 33, and is labeled like the rest until the Validator Track says otherwise.
  • How deep score history goes. Recommended default: current score, the round it was updated, and a count of submissions in the last 7 rounds. A full change log waits on the subgraph. Owner: Explorer, with the Validator Track.
  • Actions in Explorer. Register, leave, submit a score. Recommended default: leave for a later ticket once the Validator Track says Explorer is the right home. Owner: Validator Track.
  • Constitution. Expected validator behavior is still being written. Owner: Validator Track.

Accessibility and color rules: review hub.
