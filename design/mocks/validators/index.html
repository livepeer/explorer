1. Validator set #804

Filter tabs own membership. Rows stay light. Detail carries the membership pill and cadence sentence.

↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this route

  • Read-only. This ticket shows the set, status, stake, scores, and pay. It does not include registering, leaving, or filing a score. Whether those actions belong in Explorer waits on the Validator Track.
  • Set rule used here. Active set = the 33 highest-staked node operators who have registered. A higher-staked node that has not registered is “Could join”. The next registered node below the cutoff is “Next in line”. Starting size is 33. Governance can change it later.
  • Don’t repeat status three times. Filter tabs own membership. List rows stay light: name, rank, address, and one muted fact (Active / Occasional / Inactive inside the set, or why they are outside). The detail keeps one membership pill and the full cadence sentence with a help tip. Same idea as tabbed member lists and staking product tables: scope in the control, light meta in the row, full story in the pane.
  • Wide opens with the top visible validator selected. List and detail share the page. The first row in the current filter is selected on load and when the filter changes, so the detail pane is never empty on a wide screen. A deep link still wins when present.
  • Filed scores link to that node. Each name under “Scores they filed” jumps to that validator’s detail (and switches the filter if needed).
  • List is one panel. Validator rows share a single bordered sheet with a green wash and 1px ring on the selected row. Different from gateway and orchestrator tables on purpose for Livepeer 2.0, same selected-row language.
  • Blanks are “No score”. They are left out of the median in this mock, not treated as zero. See open questions.
  • Filing habit is not health. Active, Occasional, and Inactive say how often they filed scores in the last 7 rounds. Same everyday words as the tip. They appear as muted row meta and again as the detail sentence. Slate is Inactive and still paid, because the draft does not tie pay to participation.
  • Pay follows delegated stake. 1% of new LPT, weighted by delegated stake inside the set, then the node’s usual cut. Emissions are not fixed, so there is no dollar figure.
  • 2.0 is not released. Sample operators, sample stake, sample scores. Build waits on approval and on the Validator Track for the items below.

Open questions on this route

  • Words for scores that are not near 1. This sample is 0.95, so the card says honest. The draft has no bands. Recommended default: say the share of the reward in words (“keeps 40%”) and reserve “Honest” for a score at or very near 1. Owner: Validator Track. Confirm with @ECWireless.
  • What a missing score means. Recommended default: show “No score” and omit it from the median. Do not impute zero. Owner: Validator Track. Confirm with @ECWireless.
  • How the 1% is split. The parameter table weights by delegated stake, then the node’s cut. Another sentence can be read as an equal split across the set. Recommended default: stake-weighted, then the usual cut. Owner: Validator Track.
  • Health, and pay for quiet validators. Recommended default: show submission cadence only. Do not name it health, and do not zero pay, until the Validator Track defines minimum participation. Owner: Validator Track.
  • A validator scoring their own node. Recommended default: it counts like any other score, one of 33, and is labeled like the rest. Not special-cased until the Validator Track says otherwise.
  • How deep score history goes. Recommended default: current score, the round it was updated, and a count of submissions in the last 7 rounds. A full change log waits on the subgraph. Owner: Explorer, with the Validator Track.
  • Actions in Explorer. Register, leave, submit a score. Recommended default: not in this ticket, and not designed until the Validator Track says Explorer is the right home. Owner: Validator Track and @ECWireless.
  • How far this table may diverge from other persona tables. @ECWireless flagged that the set list looks different from gateway and orchestrator tables, and that dual chips felt busy. Recommended default: keep the split list/detail; no membership chips on rows (tabs + detail carry it); cadence as muted row meta inside the set; detail uses the same icon+label chip as 803 (membership + cadence). Confirm with @ECWireless.
  • The constitution. Expected validator behavior is not written. Explorer should not invent rules of conduct in copy or in a health grade. Owner: Validator Track.
