Decisions that span routes

  • Read-only. This ticket shows the set, status, stake, scores, and pay. It does not include registering, leaving, or filing a score. Whether those actions belong in Explorer waits on the Validator Track.
  • Honesty is not quality of service. The card says why the score exists: it cuts the stake-capped LPT if the work was not honest, fees are untouched, and stake cannot leave for about 21 days. The Honest pill is a subtle explorer green because a high score is a good signal. The 95%, the pill, and the bar use the same explorer green. A low score is not painted red. The 802 card is a plain panel. Its fee and stake tiles keep their colors.
  • Fit the existing Orchestrating tab. Order on the left: 802’s card, then this honesty card, then today’s stat grid (stake, status, regional scores, fees, price, cuts, reward calls, last round, treasury participation, cut history). Delegate stays in the right rail. 803 stays a line on the 802 card plus the History receipt. Do not add a Validating tab, and do not put the score list on 802’s orchestrator table.
  • 2.0 is not released. Sample operators, sample stake, sample scores. Build waits on approval and on the Validator Track for the items below.
