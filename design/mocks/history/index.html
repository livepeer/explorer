↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this mockup

  • History covers settled rounds. Live fee-vs-stake is 802. This page is the receipt for why a chosen round paid that amount.
  • Receipt title is the round number. Small “Round” label, big number. Cap status is the chip (Stake capped, Fees capped) and the wash on the receipt. Live status still uses “Room to earn”, “Full”, and “Balanced”.
  • Same layout, two audiences. Node account History for operators (pool + kept cut). Your account History for delegators (your share). Same math; different last mile. The Node operator toggle is review-only, as clarified on #841.
  • Master-detail. Wide: list left, receipt right. Narrow: list → drill-in with Back.
  • Link to 802. Entry sketches stay in the 802 mock. “What would grow the next one” may point at live room on that card.
  • Out of scope here. Validator / honesty product (804) is out of scope here, except the honesty factor that already applied to a paid round. Emissions schedule / network mint size is unfinished; sample pot is illustrative. No new top-level Rewards page; Rewards is a mode inside History. Transactions is a secondary feed.
  • Rejected alternatives. Full causal UI on Orchestrating or Delegating. Putting the 802 card on History. Replacing the tx feed entirely. Home or the orchestrators list as the place for past payouts.
  • Copy and color. Lead with both amounts (LPT and USDC), then why, then the LPT derivation under a disclosure. Brand blue = fees, brand green = stake quantity. Tabular numbers. Ellipsis character …. No em dashes. No dollar APY until emissions are fixed.
  • Shared status icons with 802. Stake-capped past = trend-up (live “Room to earn”). Fees-capped past = filled circle (live “Full”). Same SVGs across tickets.
  • Two settled pots. The receipt leads with LPT and USDC for the same round. Fee share is only an input to the LPT cap (the smaller of fee share and stake share). USDC is work fees, then the split between the node and delegators. Affirmed with @ECWireless on #841 (USDC added to the receipt with icons).
  • Claim is a separate card. The round list is for reading. Claim entry is the “3 rounds of rewards ready” card (Route A): it opens a dialog that lists the rounds, then a total line for LPT and USDC, then Claim these rewards and Close. Route B (row “Ready to claim” status) is the alternate below. Working default is Route A, as replied on #841. If product later auto-distributes, the list can say paid and claim leaves this surface.
  • Round 4210 math. LPT: fee 11.8%, stake 2.3% → stake caps. Pot 40,000 LPT → 920 eligible → honesty 0.95 → pool 874 LPT → 10% cut → a 2% delegator gets 15.732 LPT. USDC, separate pot: 40.00 USDC, node kept 20.00, delegators shared 20.00, this delegator +0.40 (that same 2% of the delegators’ half). Claimable in round 4217, seven rounds later. Secondary rows use the same kind of half split (80.00 → +0.80, and 36.00 → +0.36) plus the fee-capped and honesty 1.0 LPT variants.

Open questions on this mockup

  • APY / realized return %. Ticket asked for delegator APY. Emissions are still a draft. Recommended default: show LPT amounts and share math only; hold APY until emissions are fixed. Owner: emissions / protocol.
  • History default mode. Recommended default: Rewards when the account is an orchestrator (or ?mode=rewards); Transactions when there is no node history. Confirm with people who still live in the tx feed.
