Notes by issue

1. Receipts on each profile #803

A delegator sees their own share on their profile. A node operator sees the whole pool on the node’s page. The two are never nested under one switch in the product.

↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Two frames. Transactions live in Activity. Your payouts sit on your profile and a node operator’s payouts sit on the node’s page, so there is no Rewards and Transactions switch.
  • Receipts, not a ledger. The list is rounds. The detail is why that round paid what it paid, in amounts first and math under a disclosure.
  • Claim stays under the receipts. The receipt has no claim button. A separate card opens a dialog with the rounds, the total and Claim these rewards.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Where does the claim card live? Under receipts here. It could sit on Portfolio next to unclaimed fees instead.

Shared accessibility and color rules live on the review hub.
