4. Day one checklist #802

Gateways, then fees, then stake. Brand green with the Livepeer mark as backdrop.

↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Room to earn / Full / Balanced. Those names speak to the stake choice on the compare list. Under 2.0 the old “Active” label loses its meaning with the capped set. On /delegating, the same stake-capped state reads “You’re earning” instead: the user already bonded, so the copy is about their stake, not leftover room for new stake.
  • Day one is a checklist. The launch card names where work comes from (gateways), then fees, then stake, with brand green and the Livepeer mark as the backdrop.
  • About 21 days to move. That is the working assumption here. If the protocol keeps instant transfers, the note comes out before ship. The wording is ready to follow the rule.
  • History is 803. This ticket owns live fee-vs-stake status. Past payouts and “why that round paid that amount” live on account History · Rewards ( 803). Orchestrating only carries a week-later handoff line.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Moving stake (~21 days). Design assumes a delayed move so stake cannot flee a penalized node. If moving stake stays instant, delete the lockup note rather than leave a lie. Flag for protocol.
  • APY / dollar mint. Emissions are still a draft. Recommended default: show share math and ceiling movement (for example 2.3% toward 11.8%). Do not ship APY, “5× daily rewards,” or a dollar mint until emissions are fixed.
  • Collapsed cut breakdown. Whether stake still earns is the open question on Orchestrating. How the cut divides waits until someone is ready to commit. Confirm that collapsed-by-default is the right default for stakeholders.

5. Score sheet #804

Full set of scores opens in a sheet. No arbitrary five-score preview on the card.

↑ Show in mock

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Honesty is not quality of service. The card says why the score exists: it cuts the stake-capped LPT if the work was not honest, fees are untouched, and stake cannot leave for about 21 days. The Honest pill is a subtle explorer green because a high score is a good signal. The 95%, the pill, and the bar use the same explorer green. A low score is not painted red. The 802 card is a plain panel. Its fee and stake tiles keep their colors.
  • Both directions in the ticket. “How it was scored” is the scores on this node, and the 95% is the median of those. “How it scored others” is what this validator gave other nodes. Copy stays generic so it does not need the node’s name.
  • 95%, not 9.5 out of 10. The number is the fraction of the stake-capped reward a delegator keeps. A ten-point grade would read as quality. The protocol’s 0 to 1 figure stays in the eng appendix, not under the percent. The card tells you whether the room above is still worth delegating into: at 95% the LPT survives, fees are untouched, and only a score near zero strands stake for about 21 days.
  • The full set of scores opens in a sheet. The card does not preview five of them. Five is an arbitrary sample and it collapses once a node has hundreds of scores. The sheet plots every score on a fixed 0 to 100 axis, and again across the week before pay. Height or position is the score. The axis does not rescale to the busiest value. There is no rolling cap. The median on file at pay is what counts. Past rounds that already paid stay on 803.
  • Fit the existing Orchestrating tab. Order on the left: 802’s card, then this honesty card, then today’s stat grid (stake, status, regional scores, fees, price, cuts, reward calls, last round, treasury participation, cut history). Delegate stays in the right rail. 803 stays a line on the 802 card plus the History receipt. Do not add a Validating tab, and do not put the score list on 802’s orchestrator table.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Words for scores that are not near 1. This sample is 0.95, so the card says honest. The draft has no bands. Recommended default: say the share of the reward in words (“keeps 40%”) and reserve “Honest” for a score at or very near 1. Owner: Validator Track. Confirm with @ECWireless.
  • What a missing score means. Recommended default: show “No score” and omit it from the median. Do not impute zero. Owner: Validator Track. Confirm with @ECWireless.
