Three steps for an operator who just registered. Fresh nodes stay off the default list until fees land.

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Same profile as the Explorer. Stat tiles, performance chart and reward calls match the live page. The fee and stake card is the one addition.
  • Plain state names. “Stake-limited,” “Fee-limited,” “Balanced.” They describe the node, not the delegator’s return, so the estimate sits right under the header.
  • No broadcasting tab. On the new Explorer, broadcasting is the Gateways page. This profile is about orchestrating only.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Delegate placement. The header button and the Estimate card both go to the same amount box. Keep one?

Design decisions on this mockup

  • Measured first. A delegator wants results over time, so the section leads with calls, fees and cut changes. The validator score is one measured row among them.
  • One plain verdict. “Looks honest” is backed by the sentence under it, so it is not color alone.

Open questions on this mockup

  • Is a validator median the right signal? Whether it tells a delegator enough is a protocol question. This mock keeps it as one row and leans on measured results. A product decision, not a layout one.
