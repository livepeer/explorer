Design decisions

  • History is past; Orchestrating is now. 802 owns the live fee-vs-stake card. This ticket owns settled rounds and a receipt for why a chosen round paid that amount. Do not move the live card onto History.
  • Receipt title is the round number. “Round” is the quiet label. The number is the title. Cap status is the chip (Stake capped, Fees capped) and the wash on the receipt. “Room to earn”, “Full”, and “Balanced” stay on live status.
  • One shell, two homes. Node account History for operators (pool + kept cut). Your account History for delegators (your share). Same math; different last mile. The mock toggle is review-only.
  • Master-detail, not stacked. Wide: list left, receipt right. Narrow: list → drill-in with Back. Rejected: full receipt above a list when two columns fit.
  • Do not reprint 802. Entry sketches stay in the 802 mock. Link only. “What would grow the next one” may point at live room; it must not impersonate the live status card.
  • Out of scope here. Validator / honesty product (804) stays out, except showing the historical honesty factor that already applied to a paid round. Emissions schedule / network mint size is unfinished; sample pot is illustrative. No new top-level Rewards page; Rewards is a mode inside History. Transactions stays as a demoted feed, not deleted.
  • Rejected alternatives. Full causal UI on Orchestrating or Delegating (collapses decide-now with archaeology, or retells the node story on every bonded account). Gluing the 802 card onto History. Replacing the tx feed entirely. Home / orchestrators list as the home for past payouts.
  • Copy and color. Lead with both amounts (LPT and USDC), then why, then the LPT derivation under a disclosure. USDC does not use those steps. Brand blue = fees, brand green = stake (quantity, not “good”). Never amber for neutral protocol states. Tabular numbers. Ellipsis character …. No em dashes. No dollar APY until emissions are fixed.
  • Shared status icons with 802. Stake-capped past = trend-up (live “Room to earn”). Fees-capped past = filled circle (live “Full”). Same SVGs; do not invent per-ticket icons.
  • Two settled pots. The receipt leads with LPT and USDC for the same round. Fee share is only an input to the LPT cap (the smaller of fee share and stake share). USDC is work fees, then the split between the node and delegators. It is not reduced by stake and does not appear in the LPT steps.
  • The receipt is not a claim form. History is the past record. Claim is the card under the mock, “3 rounds of rewards ready”. It opens a dialog that lists the rounds, then a total line for LPT and USDC, then Claim these rewards and Close. A “Ready to claim” status on sample rows is the other option.
  • Round 4210 math. LPT: fee 11.8%, stake 2.3% → stake caps. Pot 40,000 LPT → 920 eligible → honesty 0.95 → pool 874 LPT → 10% cut → a 2% delegator gets 15.732 LPT. USDC, separate pot: 40.00 USDC, node kept 20.00, delegators shared 20.00, this delegator +0.40 (that same 2% of the delegators’ half, not the network stake cap). Claimable in round 4217, seven rounds later. Secondary rows use the same kind of half split (80.00 → +0.80, and 36.00 → +0.36) plus the fee-capped and honesty 1.0 LPT variants.

Open questions

  • APY / realized return %. Ticket asked for delegator APY. Emissions are still a draft. Recommended default: show LPT amounts and share math only; do not ship APY until emissions are fixed. Flag for @ECWireless.
  • Claim vs auto-distribute. History stays the archive. A round can still need a claim about seven rounds after it settles. Two options are mocked under the primary view. Route A (recommended): “3 rounds of rewards ready” opens a dialog to claim the LPT and USDC together (+62.532 LPT and +1.56 USDC across the three sample rounds). Route B: a “Ready to claim” status on the round row. Recommended default: Route A. Flag for @ECWireless. If product later auto-distributes, the list can say paid and claim leaves this surface.
  • Two pots on one receipt. The receipt shows USDC and LPT for the same round (4210: +15.732 LPT and +0.40 USDC for the delegator; 874 LPT and 40.00 USDC for the node, of which the node kept 20.00 USDC). Confirm with @ECWireless that both belong on this receipt, and that USDC is not run through the stake cap.
  • History default mode. Recommended default: Rewards when the account is an orchestrator (or ?mode=rewards); Transactions when there is no node history. Confirm with people who still live in the tx feed.
  • Moving stake (~21 days). Stays an 802 copy concern, not a column on this surface.

Who can use this

These screens should work for people who cannot rely on color alone, who navigate with a keyboard, who use a screen reader, or who prefer less motion. Same rules apply when this ships in the product.

  • Color is not the only cue. Cap chips use a word, an icon, and a border. The round list is one panel. The selected row uses a wash and a 1px green ring. Fee and stake shares are also written out as numbers.
  • Keyboard. History mode, reader, and the round list are real choice groups (arrow keys, Home / End). Focus rings stay visible so you always know where you are.
  • Screen readers. Choosing a round is announced politely (round, which side capped, amount). On a phone, opening a receipt moves focus into it; Back restores focus to the selected round.
  • Help without a mouse. The “?” tips open when you tab to them, not only on hover. Targets are large enough for touch.
  • Claim sheet. “3 rounds of rewards ready” opens a dialog. The LPT and USDC marks are named. Escape or Close dismisses it and returns focus to that card. The actions use the design-system buttons and are 45px tall.
  • Motion. If the system asks for reduced motion, the page does not smooth-scroll.
