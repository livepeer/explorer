Why round 4210 paid this

Round 4210 · capped by stake

That round this node earned 11.8% of network fees and held 2.3% of stake. Rewards follow the smaller number, so they capped at 2.3%. At payout the honesty factor was 0.95, so the pool was 95% of that stake-capped amount.

Worked in round 4210 · Paid in round 4217 Rewards for round R become claimable at R+7. The honesty factor used is the median score at claim time, so a one-round fee spike can still be zeroed before payout.
